## Project Overview

The E-commerce Customer Behavior Dataset was analysed in Power BI to understand customer shopping behaviour, spending patterns and product preferences.

The project focused on preparing customer data, developing business KPIs, analysing demographic and financial patterns, and creating an interactive customer insights dashboard.

## Dataset

The analysis used the **E-commerce Customer Behavior Dataset** from Kaggle.

The dataset contains customer demographic information, shopping frequency, spending behaviour, product preferences, financial status and brand perception.

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared before analysis to ensure consistency and accuracy.

Key preparation steps included:

- Reviewed data types and dataset structure
- Checked for missing values and inconsistencies
- Standardised fields for analysis
- Prepared demographic, purchasing and preference attributes for reporting
- Created calculated fields and measures required for the dashboard

## Analysis & KPIs

The dashboard uses calculated measures to monitor customer behaviour and purchasing patterns.

**Key KPIs**

- Total Survey Respondents
- Average Shopping Frequency
- Average Customer Spending
- Most Preferred Product Category
- Brand vs Cost Preference Rate

**Analysis**

- Product category preferences
- Customer spending by city tier
- Shopping frequency by age group
- Purchase frequency by financial status
- Product preferences by gender
- Customer distribution across city tiers and states

## Interactive Dashboard

The Power BI report provides an interactive view of customer behaviour and consumer preferences.

The dashboard includes:

- Customer behaviour KPI cards
- Product category analysis
- Spending analysis by city tier
- Shopping frequency by age group
- Purchase frequency by financial status
- Product preferences by gender

Interactive filters allow users to explore customer behaviour across different demographic and purchasing attributes.

## Key Findings

**Product Preference**  
**Fashion** was the most preferred category, with **18 customers**.

**Purchase Preferences**  
Customers showed a stronger preference for **cost over brand**, at **60% vs 40%**.

**Shopping Behaviour**  
Average shopping frequency was **2 purchases per month**, with similar frequency across most age groups.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritise Fashion products**  
   Focus product promotions and campaigns around the strongest category preference.

2. **Emphasise value in marketing**  
   Highlight competitive pricing and value for money, given the stronger cost preference.

3. **Target by customer profile**  
   Tailor campaigns by age group and financial status to reflect differences in product preferences and shopping behaviour.
